### **Discover Cambridge University: A Tour of History and Heritage**

Embark on a fascinating **walking tour of Cambridge University**, led by a current student who will provide unique insights into the city's rich history and student traditions. The highlight of the tour is undoubtedly **King’s College Chapel**, renowned for its **stunning fan-vaulted ceiling**—the largest in the world—and beautiful **stained-glass windows**.

You’ll also visit iconic spots like the **Mathematical Bridge** and **Senate House**, while learning about key moments in scientific history, from **Isaac Newton** to modern breakthroughs at Cambridge’s renowned laboratories.

#### **King’s College Chapel: Architectural Wonder**

The chapel, established in 1441 by **King Henry VI**, stands as a Gothic marvel with the world’s largest **fan-vaulted ceiling** and intricate stained-glass windows depicting biblical scenes. One of the chapel’s treasures is the painting **The Adoration of the Magi** by **Peter Paul Rubens**, alongside historical elements like the wooden organ screen featuring the initials of **Henry VIII** and **Anne Boleyn**.

Visitors rave about this majestic building, with one review noting:  
*"Our guide was a current student at Cambridge and shared fascinating insights into both the university’s history and student life. The visit to King’s College Chapel was the highlight—its beauty is beyond words!"*

#### **What to Expect on the Tour**

This tour is a deep dive into **Cambridge University’s 800-year history**, including stories of notable alumni such as **Isaac Newton**, **Charles Darwin**, and **Stephen Hawking**. You will walk past some of the city’s most iconic colleges, explore famous bridges like **The Mathematical Bridge**, and learn about the city’s academic traditions.

Expect to:

- **Walk the historic streets** of the city center, passing by attractions like **The Senate House** and the **Corpus Clock**.
- Get insider perspectives from **current students**, who provide anecdotes about university life and famous traditions like May Ball.
- **Optional entry** to King’s College Chapel allows you to explore its magnificent architecture and historical treasures.

#### **Walking Route Highlights**

During the tour, expect to visit or pass by:

- **King's College Chapel:** One of the most famous chapels in the world.
- **Mathematical Bridge:** A quirky wooden structure believed to be constructed without nails.
- **The Senate House:** Where graduation ceremonies for Cambridge students take place.
- **The Round Church:** One of four medieval round churches in England.
- **Cambridge Market Square:** A bustling hub with local vendors selling everything from food to crafts.
